It's not just a financial tool

For most of history, the financial system had a velvet rope. Born in the right country, with the right currency and the right bank, and you got stability, access, and a fair shot. Everyone else got inflation, fees, closed doors — and lectures about pulling themselves up.

Crypto — stablecoins in particular — cut that rope.

This is a revolution in who gets to participate. A person anywhere in the world can now hold a dollar for the first time. Not a promise of a dollar. An actual dollar, on their phone, under their own control. That has never been possible before, and it matters more than any price chart.

We're honest about the problems

The space is full of noise. Thousands of tokens that shouldn't exist. Exchanges that list anything that moves. We'll say so, plainly — the industry needs to clean up its own house.

But we won't let the mess obscure the mission. The answer to bad actors was never to lock regular people out. It was to build better rails and report honestly on them.
