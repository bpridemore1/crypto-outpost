Circle Mints 250M USDC on Solana

Crypto Outpost  ·  Sept 25, 2026  ·  1 min read

Circle minted 250 million USDC on Solana on September 25, 2026, in a fresh treasury issuance.

Large mints like this typically reflect anticipated demand — market makers, exchanges, and payment flows stocking up on inventory. Solana remains one of USDC's busiest chains for transfers, so new supply landing there usually means it's about to move.

The practical read: a mint is supply meeting expected demand, not a market signal on its own. But it confirms Solana's position as a primary USDC rail.
